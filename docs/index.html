What it does

Three scanners.
One clear picture.

Point your camera at any label — food, nutrition facts, or skincare — and get a breakdown that actually makes sense.

🥦

Food Scanner

Scan any food label and see exactly what's inside. Ingredients are grouped into categories — preservatives, sugars, oils, additives — and flagged against your allergies and dietary preferences.

Ingredient breakdown Allergy flags Gut health insights
📊

Nutrition Scanner

Capture a nutrition facts panel and get a full macro and micronutrient breakdown. Adjust serving sizes, see daily value percentages, and compare two products side by side.

Macro rings Serving adjustments Side-by-side compare
✨

Personal Care Scanner

Skincare ingredient lists don't have to be a mystery. CheckMe identifies actives, humectants, emollients, fragrances, and flags anything that may irritate your specific skin type.

Irritant detection Ingredient categories Skin profile
How it works

Start scanning in
under a minute.

No account, no setup. Just open the app and go.

01

Set your profile

Add your allergies, dietary needs, skin type, and conditions. Takes less than a minute and stays entirely on your phone.

02

Scan a label

Point your camera at any ingredient list or nutrition panel. CheckMe reads it instantly — no internet needed.

03

See what matters

Get a clear breakdown with personal flags for things that apply to you specifically — not generic warnings.

04

Build your history

Every scan is saved locally. Browse past scans, re-analyse with an updated profile, or compare products.

🔐

Your data doesn't
go anywhere

CheckMe uses Apple Intelligence to do all its analysis directly on your iPhone. Nothing you scan — no ingredient lists, no health profile, no results — ever leaves your device.
